# Terms of use

Last updated: 9 October 2026.

This is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any difference between this text and the Portuguese version, the Portuguese version prevails.

By using Voto Lá Fora, you accept these rules.

## The site

- It is a noticeboard of rides and places to stay for getting to your polling station in the 2nd round, on 25 October 2026.
- It does not transport anyone, host anyone or act as an intermediary. Each person is responsible for what they arrange and what they do.
- It does not check identity, cars, insurance or homes, and does not guarantee that a ride or a stay will happen.
- It may go offline or have errors. Always have a plan B for getting to your polling station.

## Who can use it

- Only people aged 18 or over. Minors may travel or stay only with their father, mother or legal guardian, and the person making the request must say so in the message.
- Use only your own, true details.

## Rules

- **The site only connects people.** It does not receive payments or act as an intermediary for them. Any arrangement, including sharing trip costs, is made directly between the people involved, privately, person to person. Amounts do not appear in listings.
- **Rides only in the driver's own car or van.** No chartered coaches, taxis, ride-hailing cars or hired drivers.
- **Travel groups only on regular public transport,** such as trains or scheduled buses. Everyone buys their own ticket: nobody pays for anyone else's trip. No chartered coaches, hired vans or transport paid for the group.
- **Whoever drives** holds a driving licence valid in the country and up-to-date insurance, and does not carry more people than there are seat belts.
- **Stays only in the home where you live.** No hotels, hostels or commercial accommodation.
- **A place never depends on how anyone votes.** It is forbidden to make a ride or stay conditional on a vote, party, candidate or political view, to choose or refuse someone for that reason, and to offer or accept any advantage linked to voting. Such conduct may be an electoral crime under Brazilian law (Electoral Code, art. 299, and Lei 6.091/1974), and whoever commits it is personally liable for it.
- **No campaigning.** During the ride and the stay, nobody carries campaign material, asks for votes or asks who the other person votes for.
- **On the 25th**, a voting preference may only be expressed individually and silently. Do not arrive at the polling station as a group wearing matching clothes or carrying flags.
- **Listings without advertising or contact details:** no candidates, parties, candidate numbers, slogans, phone numbers, email addresses, links or full street addresses.
- **No discrimination.** The only exception is the "women only" option, which exists for safety. Anyone who ticks "I am a woman" declares that they are a woman.
- **The WhatsApp number you receive** may be used only to arrange that particular trip or stay. No groups, lists or advertising.
- **It is also forbidden** to insult, threaten or harass anyone, to create fake listings or requests, and to use bots.

## Moderation and reports

- Listings go live immediately, without prior review. Moderation may review them at any time, especially when they are reported.
- We may remove listings and requests that break these rules and ban anyone who persists. In serious cases, such as scams, harassment or threats, the ban is immediate.
- To report something, use the "Report" button on the listing or write to contato@votolafora.com.br.
- The site is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call 112 in the European Union, or the emergency number of the country where you are.

- The [Privacy policy](/en/sobre/privacidade) explains what data we use and how to exercise your rights.
- Listings remain public until they are cancelled or removed. You can delete your account whenever you want.

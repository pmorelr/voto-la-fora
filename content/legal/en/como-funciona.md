# How it works

Voto Lá Fora is a noticeboard. It brings together people with a spare seat in their car or a place to sleep and people who need to get to their polling station for the 2nd round, on Sunday 25 October 2026. The site is open to any voter: nobody asks who you vote for. It only connects people and does not act as an intermediary for payments. Whatever needs to be arranged stays between the person offering and the person requesting.

The site is not responsible for any matters relating to transport, accommodation, payments or confirmations between users. All interactions and dealings between users take place privately and independently, without any involvement, intermediation or influence from the platform. The conversation happens on WhatsApp.

## I want to go and vote

1. Choose the country and the city where you vote.
2. Browse the rides and the places to stay. You do not need to sign up just to look.
3. Request a place with your first name, WhatsApp number, number of people and a message. Your email address is optional and is used to send you the reply.
4. If the person confirms, they will contact you on WhatsApp. If there is no reply within 48 hours, the request expires.

## I want to help

1. Choose the country and publish a ride or a place to stay.
2. Sign in with your first name and email address. You receive a 6-digit code, with no password.
3. Each time someone sends a request, you get an email. In "My listings", you confirm or decline it.
4. Once you confirm, a "Message on WhatsApp" button appears. The number of places available goes down automatically.

## Notify me

No offers for your city yet? Leave your email address and your city. Once you confirm via the link, you will receive at most one email a day with new offers.

## What is public

- **Everyone sees** the first name of the person offering and the details of the ride or the place to stay.
- **Only the person offering sees** the first name, the number of people and the message of the person requesting. The WhatsApp number appears only once the place is confirmed.
- **Nobody sees** email addresses, surnames, street addresses or the phone number of the person offering, which the site does not even ask for.

All personal data is deleted by 15 November 2026.
